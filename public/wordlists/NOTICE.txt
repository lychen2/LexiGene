LexiGene word lists — sources and licences
==========================================

The JSON files in this directory are generated by tools/build-wordlists.py
except idioms.json, which is written by hand.

English exam lists (kaoyan.json, ielts.json, toefl.json, gre.json)
------------------------------------------------------------------
Source: ECDICT — https://github.com/skywind3000/ECDICT
Licence: MIT
Derived by keeping only entries tagged ky / ielts / toefl / gre / cet6 and
removing the CET-4 band, Oxford-3000 and Collins 4–5 words, everything within
the 4,000 most frequent words, inflected forms and proper nouns.

Japanese lists (ja-n1.json, ja-n2.json)
---------------------------------------
Source: OpenJLPT — https://github.com/evanclan/OpenJLPT
Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0
OpenJLPT is itself derived from JMdict and KANJIDIC2 (Electronic Dictionary
Research and Development Group, CC BY-SA 4.0), Jonathan Waller's JLPT lists
(tanos.co.uk, CC BY), and Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR). Only the headword, kana
reading and level are kept here; that subset remains under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Chinese lists (zh-hsk5.json, zh-hsk6.json)
------------------------------------------
Source: complete-hsk-vocabulary — https://github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary
Licence: MIT
Only the simplified headword and pinyin are kept.
